Submit your startup

Launch your project on SubmitHunt, the best Product Hunt alternative, and get a DR 49+ backlink. Sign in to start your submission.

How it works

  1. Your product. Enter your website URL and AI fills almost every field — name, tagline, description, categories, tags, pricing, target audience, tech stack, social links, SEO metadata, FAQ, and more. You review the draft before submitting: startup name, tagline, slug, category, up to 5 tags, logo, cover image, your X username, and optional LinkedIn and GitHub links.
  2. Plan & launch. Choose the Free, Premium or Featured plan and pick your launch day. Premium and Featured are paid through Stripe and go live on the weekday you choose — right after payment, or automatically on a scheduled date. Free launches join the queue for the date you select.

Launching for free

  • No payment method needed. Unlock free submission by upvoting 3 products you like, so the community knows you before you launch.
  • Free launches go live Monday to Friday, with up to 6 free slots per day.
  • Place our badge on your homepage or footer to claim a permanent DR 49+ do-follow backlink and a gold verified checkmark next to your listing. The link must stay do-follow — don't add rel="nofollow", "sponsored" or "ugc".
  • A verified badge unlocks launch dates within the next 7 days. Without one, free launches wait at least 1 week in the queue.
  • You can skip the badge and launch with a no-follow link and no checkmark, then add the badge later from your dashboard.
  • Each website, including its subpages and subdomains, gets one free launch.

Plans at a glance

  • Standard Launch — Free: live on the homepage for 7 days, badge for top 3 ranking, dofollow backlink with a verified badge; launch delayed by ~1 week without one.
  • Premium Launch — $20 per launch (one-time payment): skip the queue and launch immediately, live on the homepage for 14 days, guaranteed dofollow backlink, featured in our newsletter.
  • Featured Spot — $50 one-time: 7 days of featured placement in the feed, colorful gradient border, high visibility to daily visitors, guaranteed dofollow backlink, no subscription.
